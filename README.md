# Retail Sales Analysis using SQL & Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing retail sales data using SQL and Power BI to identify business trends, customer purchasing patterns, and sales performance.

The project involves performing SQL-based data analysis to extract meaningful insights from the dataset and developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to present the findings through visualisations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The main objective is to understand retail business performance and convert raw sales data into meaningful, data-driven insights.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse retail sales data using SQL.
- Understand overall sales and business performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Analyse sales performance across different regions.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns based on age groups.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Develop a Power BI dashboard to visualise important business insights.

## Technologies and Tools Used

- **SQL:** Used for querying, analysing, and extracting insights from the retail dataset.
- **Power BI:** Used for developing an interactive dashboard and visualising business performance.
- **Data Analysis:** Used to identify trends, patterns, and meaningful business insights.
- **Data Visualisation:** Used to present analytical findings through charts and graphs.

## Key Analysis Areas

- **Sales Analysis:** Understanding overall sales performance.
- **Product Analysis:** Identifying products with the highest sales.
- **Category Analysis:** Comparing revenue across different product categories.
- **Regional Analysis:** Analysing sales performance across geographical regions.
- **Customer Analysis:** Understanding purchasing patterns across customer age groups.
- **Monthly Sales Analysis:** Identifying monthly sales trends and changes over time.

## Key Business Insights

The analysis helped identify the following findings:

- **Top Revenue-Generating Category:** Beauty.
- **Top-Selling Product:** Bread.
- **Highest-Performing Region:** Central.
- **Highest-Contributing Customer Age Group:** 35–44 years.
- **Highest Monthly Sales:** January 2024, with sales of 1,980,147.17.
- **Lowest Monthly Sales:** December 2025, with sales of 1,871,794.42.

These findings help highlight important patterns in product performance, regional sales, customer behaviour, and monthly business performance.
